Why it sticks

  • Calendar import is the retention hook — it lives where students already look
  • Every semester = new syllabi = re-engagement, by design
  • Cited answers build trust; refusals prevent the "AI invented my deadline" failure

Unit economics

  • Extraction: one LLM call per syllabus (~$0.02), cached forever
  • Hosting: ~$20/mo serves thousands of students
  • ~3–5% premium conversion covers costs at ~1,500 active students

Go-to-market

  • Start: free launch on campus subreddits & Discords (zero CAC)
  • Grow: student government pilots → campus licenses
  • Scale: LMS/tutoring partnerships for distribution
