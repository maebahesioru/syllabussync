## COURSE OVERVIEW
CS 101 introduces programming with Python: variables, control flow, functions, data
structures, files, and an introduction to debugging and testing. No prior experience
required. This syllabus contains all deadlines, policies, and grading for the semester.

- Weekly quizzes (10 total): 15%
- Midterm exam: 15%
- Final project: 25% (proposal 5%, milestone 5%, final submission 10%, demo 5%)
- Participation: 5%
